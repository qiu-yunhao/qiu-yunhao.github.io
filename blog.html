About Me

Who I am and what drives me

Hello! I'm Alex, a passionate developer, writer, and stargazer based in Shanghai. I believe that technology and creativity can together illuminate the unknown, much like stars piercing through the night sky.

This blog is my personal cosmos—a space where I share my technical explorations, philosophical musings, and artistic endeavors. Each article is a star in this constellation, a point of light in the vast digital universe.

When I'm not coding or writing, you'll find me on my rooftop with a telescope, in a café with a notebook, or hiking in the mountains chasing clear skies.

Feel free to reach out if you'd like to collaborate, discuss ideas, or just talk about the cosmos!
